Figure 5 candidate model panels (reduced model)

The compact four-parameter main-text preset is used throughout, without refitting.
M is orange (#ED8630); E is blue (#2024F6).

seasonal_profiles: Normalized phase-derived transcription proxies for PDF loss
at 12L:12D and WT at 12L:12D, 10L:14D and 16L:8D. Gray indicates darkness.
The proxy is max(0, -cos(phi))^4, normalized separately to unit peak. These
waveforms illustrate timing; amplitudes and waveform shapes are not fitted
RNA measurements. Dashed orange exposes the coincident M/E PDF-loss curves.

peak_times and phase_gap: Lines show stationary WT model predictions; points
and error bars show stored experimental peak estimates and SDs. Phase-gap SDs
are the stored paired gap SDs, not an independent combination of phase SDs.
The paired WT phase RMSE across 11 photoperiods is 0.229 h. Empirical
photoperiod landmarks and fixed reference constants remain data-informed.

genotypes: Open circles show experimental calibration targets; filled squares
show model predictions at 12L:12D. Horizontal offsets expose overlapping
symbols without changing peak times. No measured genotype uncertainty is
supplied, so no genotype error bars are shown.

model_summary_2x2: Combined schematic, phase gap, peak times and genotype
comparison. The schematic represents shared light attraction, symmetric
synchronization and an effective PDF-dependent separating drive. Its arrows
are effective phase influences, not a claim about anatomical connectivity.
This composite preserves the legacy +/-0.07 h display-only vertical offset
for coincident PDF-loss M/E symbols; mention that offset if using the composite.
Standalone genotypes uses horizontal separation and needs no vertical-offset note.

PDF and SVG are vector exports for manuscript assembly; PNGs are 400 dpi.
The composite is 4.0 x 3.6 inches. Model peak tables, normalized profiles and
source hashes are included as CSV/JSON. Existing baseline model assets are preserved.
The source workbook Figure4_extracted.xlsx is absent in this workspace;
raw expression panels have therefore not been regenerated.
